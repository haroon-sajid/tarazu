FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Tarazu and how it transforms your audit workflow.

Does Tarazu replace our audit methodology?
No — it executes your existing methodology faster. Templates and materiality thresholds are configured to your firm’s standards.
Where is client data stored?
Encrypted at rest and in transit, in your chosen region, with role‑based access and full activity logs.
How long does onboarding take?
Most firms run their first live engagement within two weeks, with a dedicated implementation lead.
Does it integrate with our existing tools?
Yes — Tarazu offers a robust API and native integrations with n8n, Zapier, and popular ERPs.
Is it suitable for small firms?
Absolutely. The platform scales from sole practitioners to large global firms, with flexible pricing.
Is it SOC 2 compliant?
Yes, Tarazu is SOC 2 Type II ready, with regular third‑party audits and continuous monitoring.
